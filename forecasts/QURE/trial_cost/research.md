## 2. Phase I/II cost [I]
- External AMT-130 FY21–FY25 = 103.8; ex BLA prep 19.4 → **84.4M** for ~57 treated patients (26 US incl. sham-controlled, 13 EU, 12 cohort 3, 6 cohort 4) plus ongoing 5-yr follow-up → **~$1.5M external per treated patient**. Add pre-2021 start-up (not extracted) and an unknown share of unallocated staff/manufacturing: an all-in Ph I/II cost of roughly **$100–150M** is plausible, but the upper part is unverified.
- Run rate while dosing: **$10–20M/yr external**. Implies the trial is dominated by fixed/long-follow-up cost on a small n, not per-dose cost.

## 4. Conclusion and recommendation
- Model's $70M/yr ($350M) is ~3.5x my base and ~2x my high. Recommend **incremental $20M/yr FY27E–FY31E (~$100M)**; low $11M/yr, high $32M/yr.
- Double-counting: Q2 2026 run rate already holds $6.3M/qtr AMT-130 external (~$25M/yr), mostly Ph I/II follow-up, BLA/MAA prep and validation. As BLA prep falls away, part of that budget rolls into the confirmatory study. If the model compounds Q2 2026 R&D at 1.03^n, a fair incremental add is **~$15M/yr base** (≈$10–25M range).
- Confidence: low-moderate. Disclosed history is solid; vector COGS and procedure costs are the largest unsourced inputs (±$40M on the total).

## 6. Reconciliation: my bottom-up vs Kapusta bot
- **Why his $/patient is higher.** He divides $120M by 57 patients, about $2.1M each. Not all of that recurs:
  - ~$9.8M in FY17–18 was preclinical/IND work.
  - The Phase I/II has ~205 patient-years of follow-up and imaging so far [I: US 26 × ~5y, EU 13 × ~3.5y, cohort 3 12 × ~2y, cohort 4 6 × ~1y].
  - Fixed trial overhead ran over 7 years across 2 countries and 4 cohorts, which is high per patient at n = 57.
  - The US cohorts used imitation (sham) surgery.
  - Calibration [I]: $110M ex-BLA/IND, less ~$30M fixed (6y × $5M), less ~$31M follow-up (205 pt-yr × $150k), leaves ~$49M ÷ 57 ≈ **$0.85M per dosing event** (excluding vector, which was made in-house and is not in "direct" cost).
  - This is the ceiling for non-vector cost per treated patient. First-in-human safety intensity should fall in a Phase 3.
- **Why mine was too low.** My earlier $150k procedure + $100k/pt clinical understated site, surgical and follow-up intensity relative to this calibration. Orphan benchmarks are also $2–5.7M per patient, though they include small-batch manufacturing.
