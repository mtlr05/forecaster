## 2. Business Description and Reporting Lines

uniQure is a gene-therapy company. Almost all of its revenue today is royalties on Hemgenix, the haemophilia B gene therapy licensed to CSL Behring [A21]. AMT-130 for Huntington's disease (HD) is a one-time AAV5 gene therapy given by MRI-guided neurosurgery, delivered with ClearPoint Neuro's system. Its BLA and a UK MAA were filed on 2 September 2026 [G12]. The reported lines are license (royalty), collaboration and contract manufacturing revenue. We add AMT-130 product lines for the US and, in bull only, the UK. Genezen makes AMT-130 and gives it preferential access to its plant [G13].

## 5. Business Drivers by Line

Base starts at 30 centers and 1.0 procedure a week, rising to 2.0. Utilisation is 50% in the first quarter and reaches 100% after a year. Centers grow by 3 a quarter while patients are waiting, capped at 70. The cap does not bind. HDSA has 69 HD centers [A12]. ClearPoint targets 35–40 initial centers with 5,000 patients a year of capacity across all its partners [G2]. We assume vector supply does not bind through FY2029E.

## 6. Risks

**Center count.** The user's 30 compares with 15 qualified by ClearPoint in August 2026 [G1]. Fewer centers or lower utilisation push treatments later. They do not change the total.

**Refill.** If refill is 30% of incidence, it falls to 490 patients a year, and FY2031E base revenue falls to $929M. This is the key open question (Figure 5).

**Approval timing.** If approval comes in Q4 2027, FY2027E base revenue is $420M and the backlog clears one quarter later (Figure 5).

**Payer budget.** Base US spend peaks near $8.9B in FY2029E, a single year of backlog clearance. That invites managed access.

**Consensus.** FY2027E base revenue is 9.4× consensus. It is not scaled, because the user's capacity instruction overrides consensus [C202]. Goldman's reported peak is $2.5B [E6].

## 8. Valuation – NOT A VALUATION

**Finagle flagged all three scenarios `not_a_valuation: true`. The per-share figures below are mechanical outputs of default policies that misfire for this company, not values we stand behind [D30].** Raw values per share (FCFE, equal to DDM; no post-run adjustment): base $226.87, bull $663.39, bear -$18.51, vs. a price of $25.30 on 2026-10-30 [D30]. The v3 run on the older forecast gave $97.40 / $221.53 / −$18.09 [D32].

Reasons, in Finagle's words:

- **base:** default SBC (year-0 SBC/EBITDA ratio) goes negative: min -798.9; negative cash (unfunded; no funding policy); HemB SPV royalty liability 473.2 not in debt (Finagle default debt 49.699) while royalties stay in EBITDA. Sanity flags: negative cash, negative FCFE after year 1. Validation warning: average EBITDA growth is 42.9% and there are no acquisitions. If that growth includes acquired EBITDA, use an organic path and book the purchase price with acquisitions, or MnA stays zero.
- **bull:** default SBC (year-0 SBC/EBITDA ratio) goes negative: min -1389.0; negative cash (unfunded; no funding policy); HemB SPV royalty liability 473.2 not in debt (Finagle default debt 49.699) while royalties stay in EBITDA. Sanity flags: negative cash, negative FCFE after year 1.
- **bear:** negative capex in forecast years; negative cash (unfunded; no funding policy); HemB SPV royalty liability 473.2 not in debt (Finagle default debt 49.699) while royalties stay in EBITDA. Sanity flags: negative cash, negative FCFE after year 1.

**Terminal growth is Finagle's default, not ours.** Finagle set terminal growth to its risk-free-based default of 4.99% [D30]. The forecast's own next-period growth is 0.08% in base, 0.02% in bull and 3.00% in bear [D31]. For a one-time therapy whose sales fall to a refill level after the backlog clears, a ~5% perpetual growth rate is far above the forecast and inflates the terminal value.

**Debt path.** The user asked to hold debt until EBITDA is positive, then target 1x EBITDA [P103]. Base debt rises with EBITDA to a peak of 6,910 in FY2029E, then falls only slowly while EBITDA drops to about 1,400. Debt is 3.6–4.9x EBITDA from FY2030E to FY2035E (max 4.9x), not 1x, and cash builds to about 13,401 and stays there [D30]. Borrowing that cash and holding it adds nothing to the operations; it shows the target is applied to rising EBITDA but not enforced on the way down. Bull peaks at 12,014 with a maximum of 1.7x; bear holds debt at 49.699 [D30].

**Policies the user has not decided** (from the request [D31]), with the risk each creates:

- **stock-based compensation policy.** Finagle's default holds the year-0 SBC/EBITDA ratio (about -11.6%, SBC 17.7 / EBITDA -152.9 in FY2025). Once EBITDA turns positive (FY2027E) that ratio gives negative SBC, i.e. a non-cash cost becomes a cash add-back and inflates value. If defaults_filled shows stock comp at that ratio, report the result as not a valuation.
- **capex / reinvestment (ROIC) policy.** Defaults tie capex to growth or year-0 ratios of a pre-revenue company; with revenue jumping ~40x then falling, implied reinvestment can be near zero or negative, overstating free cash flow.
- **HemB SPV royalty-financing liability $473.2M (10-K, FY2025).** Booked as debt (ASC 470) and repaid from Hemgenix royalties until 1.85x upfront ($693.8M) by 2032-06-30, else up to 2.25x through 2038. Not decided by David whether to count it as debt or strip royalty cash.
- **tax rate and NOL use.** uniQure has large accumulated losses (Dutch/US). A default statutory tax from FY2027E ignores NOLs (understates value); unlimited NOLs overstate it. Not decided.
- **funding of negative cash (bear especially).** Bear EBITDA is negative every year with debt held; if cash runs below zero Finagle may treat it as free negative cash at no cost or dilution, misstating bear value. No equity raise or dilution policy stated (post_finagle is null).
