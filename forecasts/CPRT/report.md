## 2. Business Description and Reporting Lines

Copart runs online vehicle auctions, mainly for insurers disposing of total-loss vehicles. It sold more than 4 million units in the last year across 11 countries [A16]. Insurance sellers supplied 79% of vehicles processed in FY2026A [A9]. Copart reports two segments, U.S. and International, each split into service revenues and vehicle sales [A1].

- **Service revenues** are fees on consigned vehicles: listing, selling (fixed, tiered or a percentage of the sale price), transport, title and storage fees.
- **Vehicle sales** are the gross proceeds of cars Copart buys for its own account and resells. Margins on these are thin.

ACV Auctions is a U.S. digital dealer-to-dealer wholesale marketplace. It earns marketplace fees, service fees and customer assurance revenue [A11]. Since 16 October 2026 it has operated as an independent subsidiary [A13].

## 5. Business Drivers by Line

**U.S. service revenues (73%).** This line is units × fee per car.
- *Volume:* total-loss frequency, accident frequency and catastrophes drive units. Winter adds 5–20% to facility volumes [A8][A10]. U.S. volume fell in FY2026A, and the insurance share of units slipped from 81% to 79% [A5][A9].
- *Price:* many fees are linked to the auction price, so used-car values, scrap prices and FX for overseas buyers feed through [A8]. Revenue per car rose in FY2026A [A5].
- FY2025A included one-time revenue from hurricanes Helene and Milton [A5]. FY2026A is therefore a clean base.
- We assume +2% in FY2027E and +3% in FY2028E, with no major storm [S1].

**U.S. vehicle sales (9%).** This line is mix-driven: revenue per car rose on mix while volume fell [A6]. Cost of vehicle sales moves with it, so the EBITDA impact is small [A12]. We assume +3% a year [S2].

**International service revenues (12%).** Revenue per car, volume and sellers moving from purchase to consignment all helped [A5][A6]. FX added $21.9M in FY2026A [A5]. We assume +9% a year, with no further FX help [S3].

**International vehicle sales (6%).** The consignment shift removes volume from this line and adds fees to service revenue [A6]. We assume +1% a year [S4].

**ACV Auctions.**
- *Track record:* revenue rose from $358M to $760M over 2021–2025 [A15]. Q2 2026 revenue grew 10% on flat units, so growth is coming from fee per unit and services rather than volume [A11].
- *Guidance:* pre-close, ACV guided 2026 revenue of $845–855M and adjusted EBITDA of $73–77M [G1].
- *Our build:* we include 77 days of Q4 2026 plus seven months of 2027 consensus in FY2027E [S5][E2]. FY2028E assumes 10% growth in 2028 [S6].

## 6. Risks

**Margin erosion.** Q4 FY2026A cash gross margin fell to 46.6% and adjusted EBITDA margin to 38.2%, from 42.2% a year earlier [C3][C2]. Each 100bps of organic cash gross margin is worth about $48M of FY2027E EBITDA [C10][C3].

**Volume.** Lower total-loss frequency, falling insurer share or lower claims frequency would hit the largest line [A8][A9].

**Used-car and scrap prices.** These drive fee per car on percentage-based fees [A8].

**Integration.** ACV is Copart's largest deal. Synergies are unquantified, and integration costs and purchase accounting could change the reported ACV contribution [G3][A13].

**Dealer wholesale cycle.** ACV cited "ongoing headwinds in the dealer wholesale market" [A11].

**Catastrophes.** Storms cut both ways: they lift revenue, as in FY2025A, but they also bring one-time costs [A5][A7].

**FX.** A stronger dollar reduces both International revenue and overseas buyer demand [A5][A8].

**Dilution.** The 20M-share CEO option adds stock compensation; it is excluded from our EBITDA [A14][C2].

## Appendix C – Method

1. Copart gives no guidance, so the revenue totals are the consensus mean, low and high.
2. Each reported line (segment × service/vehicle sales) is built from its volume and price drivers as stated in the 10-K.
3. ACV is added as a separately shown acquired line, from its last guidance and consensus, mapped from calendar to Copart's July fiscal year by day count and months.
4. The lines are scaled in proportion to the consensus total.
5. Costs:
   - The organic cash gross margin is held at the trailing year.
   - G&A is held at the latest-quarter run rate.
   - ACV is added at an assumed margin.
   - One-time items (hurricane revenue and costs, deal costs) are excluded.
6. EBITDA is analyst-defined: operating income + D&A + stock compensation.
7. No valuation was run.
